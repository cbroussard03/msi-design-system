Brand Voice

MSI speaks with the authority of engineers and field veterans. We are technically credible, proudly Texan, service-minded, and cost-competitive without ever sounding cheap.

✓ Do
  • Lead with the problem we solve or the spec that matters
  • Use real numbers — pressures, sizes, certifications
  • Say "Made in Texas" and "Field Ready. Proven Quality."
  • Tag and recognize employees and partners by name
  • Show field use cases with authentic visuals
  • Use: cost-competitive · purpose-built · engineered for
✗ Don't
  • Say "cheap," "cheaper," or "budget" without context
  • Use generic stock photography when field photos exist
  • Over-explain to an audience that knows oilfield terminology
  • Sound like a press release ("we are pleased to announce")
  • Use: solutions · leverage · utilize · synergy · cutting-edge
  • Promise things we can't back up with data or engineering

Usage by Medium
Technical Documents

Colors

  • White background
  • Navy Primary for headings & section bars
  • Orange for callouts & warnings
  • Black/deepest for body text

Typography

  • H1–H3 in Kollektif Bold
  • Body at 11–12pt, 1.5–1.75 leading
  • Part numbers in Courier New

Voice

  • Precise, neutral, spec-driven
  • Engineering voice — no marketing language
Promotional Materials

Colors

  • Dark navy backgrounds (#0a0b18 / #2a2f7c)
  • Orange for CTAs and key callouts
  • White text on dark
  • Blue Medium for secondary accents

Typography

  • Display type 3.5rem+ for hero
  • Uppercase eyebrows in Orange
  • Bold, short headline copy

Voice

  • Confident, benefit-focused
  • Lead with spec or field outcome
Website

Colors

  • White default background
  • Subtle (#f4f5fb) for alternates
  • Dark footer in Navy Deepest
  • Orange for primary buttons
  • Blue Medium for links

Typography

  • Kollektif via Shopify CDN
  • Body at 1rem / 1.5 leading
  • Responsive scale with clamp()

Voice

  • Service-minded and direct
  • Spec-forward product descriptions
